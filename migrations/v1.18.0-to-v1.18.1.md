# v1.18.0 to v1.18.1

A workbench command runs until it ends; a call waits at most 600 seconds; no output is lost. This follows the
organization's timeout standard of 2026-09-30: a timeout bounds how long a call waits, never how long the work
runs.

Selected by the organization's human authority on 2026-09-30.

- `skills/workbench/SKILL.md`:
  - no command time limit unless the agent sets `deadline_seconds`;
  - `run` waits `wait_seconds` (default 120, at most 600) and answers `running` with a `run_id` when the command
    outlasts the wait;
  - `start`, `wait`, `status`, `cancel`, `output` and `runs` follow a run;
  - a `stalled` flag that never stops a command;
  - at most 30,000 characters of output per answer, with every byte kept and readable by position;
  - service logs readable whole by position;
  - the shim's `/v1/runs` routes in the HTTP fallback.

  This replaces the 60-to-600-second command window and the last-200-lines tail.
- `VERSION` 1.18.1, `manifest.json` (workbench digest; every digest re-checked against its file).
