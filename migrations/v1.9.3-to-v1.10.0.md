# v1.9.3 to v1.10.0

Gaps retain encountered absences, concerns, and uncertainties, including minor
or tentative ones. The root protocol directs agents to capture them during
assigned work, consult relevant existing Gaps, and preserve relationships to
available records. Reporting assigns no qualification, Candidate, Decision,
or remedy and does not change an Oracle result.

The Gap contract distinguishes observations from interpretations and accepts
unresolved impact. Earlier observations remain unchanged after closure; later
work may append attributed observations. State and Resolution remain with
separately assigned disposition work, supported by the existing evidence and
Decision relationships.

Oracles retain complete coverage of the Promise within Scope and gain an
explicit sufficiency judgment. Outside-Scope concerns may be recorded as Gaps
without becoming failed legs or narrowing the Promise. No namespace, runtime
gate, output schema, or additional closure pass is introduced.
