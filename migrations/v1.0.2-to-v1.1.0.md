# v1.0.2 to v1.1.0

Strengthens promise/oracle/witness assurance semantics based on the first
independent adoption evaluation.

- Oracles require per-leg Implementation coverage; partially automated
  oracles remain honest without over- or under-claiming implementation.
- Witnesses map evidence to every oracle Pass leg and forbid circular
  provenance evidence.
- Assured promises require witness coverage of the complete Promise text or
  explicit Residual exclusions.
- Completed runs require `result.json`.
- Numeric claims name and re-derive their source; provenance terms resolve to
  a path, tree, commit, or URL.
- Decisions introduced on an unmerged PR may be corrected before acceptance;
  merged accepted decisions remain append-only.
