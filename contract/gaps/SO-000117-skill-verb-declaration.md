# Gap SO117: skill verb declaration authority is ambiguous

State: unresolved.

The installed user-level protocol says: “A procedure an agent follows — a
skill — declares the one verb it performs”. The published protocol 2.0.0
archive contains thirteen verb procedure skills and five structural
procedure skills. `contract/roles.md` gives multi-verb roles: implementer
holds declare/formulate/produce/refine and validator holds judge/declare.
The published migration and compiler documentation do not explicitly say
whether organisation role/procedure skills must universally declare one
verb, or are exempt from that installed sentence.

Published coordinates are the immutable `v2.0.0` files
`contract/roles.md`, `migrations/2.0.0-draft-to-2.0.0.md` and
`compiler/README.md` in cleverunicornz/yeetz-bedrock-protocol. The accepted
scope is recorded at
https://github.com/cleverunicornz/task-graph/pull/15#issuecomment-5971704413.

This factual uncertainty grants no exemption, waiver, new act or semantic
redefinition. Its resolution is not assigned to patch compatibility.
