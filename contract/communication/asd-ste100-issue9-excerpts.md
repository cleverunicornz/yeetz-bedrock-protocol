## Rule 1.11: consistent technical nouns

> Do not use different technical nouns for the same item.

## Rule 3.2: verb forms and tenses

> Use only these verb forms and tenses of verbs:
> - The infinitive form
> - The imperative form (command form)
> - The simple present tense
> - The simple past tense
> - The simple future tense
> - The past participle form (as an adjective).

## Rule 3.6: unknown actors

> Use the active voice. In descriptive writing, you can use the passive voice only when the agent is unknown.

## Rule 5.4: conditions before instructions

> When there is a condition that the reader must know about first, start the instruction with a descriptive statement. Then, divide that descriptive statement from the command with a comma.

## Rule 9.1: explanation of meaning preservation

> When you replace a word, always make sure that the alternative that you select does not change the meaning of the sentence.
