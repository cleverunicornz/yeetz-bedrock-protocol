# Source: ASD-STE100 Issue 9

Bedrock STE100 Communication is an original adaptation of selected
conventions from **ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, Issue 9,
2025-01-15**, published by ASD STEMG.

Official source:
https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf

Supporting explanation:
https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html

The source was retrieved on 2026-10-09. Selected source excerpts and capture
metadata are retained in `contract/communication/asd-ste100-issue9-excerpts.md`.
The release manifest pins those excerpts with this profile. The edition and
rule coordinates below identify the full source independently of its text
conversion. The package publishes its own guidance and limited quotations,
not the full standard.

## Deliberate adaptations

The profile applies to conversation and knowledge records as well as
technical instructions. Sentence lengths are editing targets. It retains
Bedrock's domain vocabulary and the distinctions between evidence,
possibility, permission and commitment. It does not adopt the complete STE
dictionary or claim full ASD-STE100 conformance.

The two-sided convention review is Bedrock's use of its existing acts,
not a procedure attributed to ASD-STE100. Its counterpart cases examine
what an additional convention would impair as well as what it could prevent.

The initial investigation was prompted by
https://github.com/menonpg/ste-prompt/tree/f59ae8de5497c81ece60e0a8fac11fe6a54d3777.
That project is an inspiration reference; the official standard and the
adopted Bedrock profile are the sources for this convention.

An upstream revision is a new input to evaluate. It does not silently alter
the edition or wording selected by the protocol Decision. The release
manifest pins the profile, this source note and its review skill together.
