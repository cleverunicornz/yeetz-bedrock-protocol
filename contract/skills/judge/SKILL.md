# judge

`judge` applies a Promise's Oracle to one Witness of that Promise and records
an outcome, the verdict, with a reason. Judging is the same at every level.
The judge decides only whether the Promise as stated holds within its scope.

## Contract

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Kind | `act` |
| Acts on | `Witness` `Promise` |
| Inputs | `Promise` `Oracle` `Witness` |
| Outputs | `verdict` |
| Outcomes | `holds` `does_not_hold` `inconclusive` |
| Held by | `validator` |

## When

A Witness of the Promise exists, the Promise has an Oracle neither superseded
nor revoked, and that (Witness, Oracle) pair is not yet judged.

## The verdicts

- `holds`: the Witness shows the Promise holds within its scope. If it is the
  latest standing verdict, the Promise becomes `assured` (`assure`).
- `does_not_hold`: a demonstrated failure within scope, shown by a concrete
  probe and cited as `failure_evidence`. A hypothetical failure is never
  `does_not_hold`. An assured Promise goes back to `asserted`.
- `inconclusive`: a verdict on the Witness, not on the Promise; the Promise's
  state is unchanged. `refine` may follow.

The Witness becomes `judged` whatever the verdict.

## Bounded

- **Positive space only.** Judge the Promise as stated, within its scope. Do
  not widen the scope, add requirements, or try to prove a negative.
- **Evidence is what the Witness shows.** A claim the Witness does not show
  is not evidence; the judge may regenerate the observation to check it.
- **Gaps are declared, never gating.** A condition under which the Promise
  might not hold is a separate `declare` act with `arose_in` set to this
  judge act. Such a Gap is binding as a finding, decided only by `decide`,
  and never blocks assurance.
- **A judge holds `judge` and `declare` only.** It reports and does not fix,
  decide or mint. The fix belongs to the producer and arrives as a new
  Witness, judged anew.
- Above board level, a judge act by the producer of the judged Witness is a
  drift signal.

## Refusals

- `oracle_not_in_force`: the judgment applies the Promise's Oracle that is
  neither superseded nor revoked.
- `already_judged`: one judgment per (Witness, Oracle) pair. A rejudge happens
  only when the Witness or the Oracle is new; judging the same pair again is
  not an act.
- Judging a revoked Witness, or an Oracle or Witness of another Promise, is
  refused; so is a `does_not_hold` without its failure evidence.

## Not this verb

- Recomputing a state never creates work: no judgment is ever required or
  generated by a state change. Revoking a Witness or superseding an Oracle
  does not call for a rejudge.
- `assure` is not performed; it is the state change a `holds` verdict causes.
