# Bedrock 2.1

Use these tools at session start and throughout the work:
- Task graph maps the world: records, binding rules and unfinished work in the current scopes.
- Memory recalls what happened through transcript pointers.
- Board outlines this session and its delegated work.

The task graph's act log is the authority; records and states are its projections.
Read the applicable verb skill before an act; query the graph for binding rules and open work.

An Invariant is a rule binding its scope; states: stipulated, superseded, revoked.
A Gap is an encountered absence, concern or uncertainty; states: declared, decided, superseded, revoked.
A Candidate is an evidence-derived hypothesis for a response; states: formulated, evaluated, decided, superseded, revoked.
A Decision is a choice collapsed with its grounds; states: decided, superseded, revoked.
A Promise is a falsifiable assertion a judgment can assure; states: asserted, assured, superseded, revoked.
An Oracle is the judgment rule for one Promise; states: defined, superseded, revoked.
A Witness is an observation retained independently of judgment; states: produced, judged, revoked.
A Reference is pinned depth supporting a record; states: stored, superseded, revoked.
A Plan is a group of Candidates, Promises and Gaps, not a noun or assertion; states: grouped, regrouped, revoked.
An act is one recorded use of a performed verb or structural operation, with lineage.

Outcomes belong to acts; states follow standing acts and the Promise assurance transition.
History remains retained; procedure, edit windows and refusals belong in the skills.
Scope authority changes only through a qualified migration receipt; a release alone switches no scope.
